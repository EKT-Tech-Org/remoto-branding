# Logos

All marks are exact vectors extracted from the REMOTO 2023 brochure (page 3). Colours: R and wordmark `brand-purple` #553D9F, tile `brand-teal` #59C7C4, reversed ink `teal-soft` #7ED3CA.

- **remoto-logo-stacked** — primary. Tile above wordmark, spacing as in the brochure.
- **remoto-logo-horizontal** — tile left, wordmark right at 40% of tile height. New arrangement of the original parts.
- **remoto-logo-reversed** — mark above wordmark in teal-soft, for `indigo` / `brand-purple` grounds (matches the app splash on page 1). Shows on a transparent background here.
- **remoto-app-icon** (+ 1024px PNG) — app stores and avatars.
- **remoto-mark-purple / -teal** — R alone.
- **remoto-wordmark-purple / -white** — REMOTO alone. The white one is invisible on this page's light tiles; use it on `indigo`, `navy` or photography.
